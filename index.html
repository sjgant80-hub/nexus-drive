What "closes" means — and what it does not. This is a test of the architecture's coherence: do the control loops stabilise, does the power balance, do the interfaces between subsystems actually carry what they must? It is not a claim that the exotic physics is real. If the architecture does not even close as a coupled system, that is a finding worth having before any lab. If it does, the next question — is the physics real — is Gary's, and the gap list below says exactly where his equations replace our placeholders.
